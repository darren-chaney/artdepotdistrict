Who We Are

The Art Depot District

The Art Depot District is a growing creative and commercial corridor in Covington, Tennessee — officially recognized by the city as the Artesian Business District. The area brings together a diverse mix of arts, culture, food, agriculture, craft spirits, and light manufacturing activity.

From art studios to a farmers market, from craft distilling to vintage retail, the district represents the spirit of independent business and community investment that makes Covington unique. Every business here is part of something larger — a destination, not just a location.

The district's anchor event, Depot Days, brings the entire community together each year for a weekend celebration of everything the area has to offer.

Official City Name

The area is officially rezoned by the City of Covington as the Artesian Business District. Locally and publicly the district is known and marketed as the Art Depot District.
