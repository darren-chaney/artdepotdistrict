About Depot Days

A Weekend to Remember

Depot Days is the Art Depot District's flagship annual festival, bringing together the entire Covington community for a weekend of art, music, food, local vendors, and the famous car show. It's the best way to experience everything the district has to offer in one place.

FAQs

Is Depot Days free to attend? ▾
Yes, general admission to the Depot Days festival grounds is free. The car show has a separate registration fee for entrants.
Where do I park? ▾
Free parking is available throughout the district. Additional overflow parking details will be posted closer to the event.
Can I register my car the day of? ▾
Pre-registration is strongly encouraged so we can plan for the number of vehicles. Day-of registration may be available on a limited basis.
